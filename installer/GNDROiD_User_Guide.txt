Welcome to GNDROiD, the institutional-grade Android Virtual Machine and 
Physical Device Orchestrator for Windows 10 & 11 (64-bit).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS & PREREQUISITES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Operating System: Windows 10 (Build 19041+) or Windows 11 (64-bit)
* CPU: Intel Core or AMD Ryzen with Virtualization enabled (VT-x / AMD-V)
* RAM: 8 GB minimum (16 GB - 32 GB recommended for multi-instance workloads)
* Storage: 10 GB+ SSD recommended
* WHPX: Windows Hypervisor Platform enabled in Windows Features.
  To enable in PowerShell (Admin):
  Enable-WindowsOptionalFeature -Online -FeatureName HypervisorPlatform -All

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FIRST LAUNCH & CONFIGURATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Start GNDROiD via the Desktop Shortcut or by launching gndroid.exe.
* On first start, GNDROiD automatically detects your host CPU cores, RAM, 
  and GPU, provisioning a flagship Android instance.
* Click [Launch Android Machine] to boot the VM in a realistic device window.
* Use the Hardware Controls Ribbon on the right for Back, Home, Recents, 
  Volume, Rotation, and Screenshot capture.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. VIRTUAL MACHINE HARDWARE ALLOCATION (SETTINGS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click the [Settings] button on the main window:
* Hardware & CPU/RAM: Customize RAM (2 GB to 32 GB), Dedicated Cores (2 to 24), 
  ART heap size, and optional Root Access (Writable System).
* Graphics & Display: Auto Vulkan 1.4 / Direct3D 11 hardware passthrough, 
  resolutions up to Galaxy S24 Ultra (1440x3120 @ 500 DPI) and 4K displays.
* Camera & Audio: Route host PC webcams and microphones directly into Android 
  apps for WhatsApp video calls, identity KYC, and streaming.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TRI-MODE NETWORK ADAPTER & HARDWARE BRIDGING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under Settings -> Network & Adapters:
* Mode 1 (High-Speed LAN Bridge): Maps emulator ports (51888, 5555, 8080) to 
  your host PC IP (e.g. 192.168.100.5) with zero driver requirements.
* Mode 2 (Hardware Bridged - Default): Bridges the VM directly to your 
  physical Wi-Fi 6 or Ethernet adapter. Your home router assigns an independent 
  192.168.100.x IP address directly to Android with unique virtual MAC.
* Direct Static IP & Custom DNS: Assign static IPv4 addresses and select 
  Google (8.8.8.8), Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), or Quad9 DNS servers. Click [Apply] 
  to inject live without rebooting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MULTI-INSTANCE MANAGER & CLONING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click [Multi-Instance Manager & Clones]:
* Create and run multiple Android instances simultaneously.
* Each instance receives a unique deterministic MAC and sequential router IP.
* 1-Click clone preserves all installed applications, user data, and logins.
* [Tile Windows] automatically arranges all active VMs across your monitors.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PHYSICAL PHONE MIRRORING & APP CLONING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* USB Mirroring: Turn on USB Debugging on your phone, plug USB cable, and 
  click [Mirror Phone] for zero-latency 60 FPS streaming with host audio.
* Wireless Debugging: Click [Wireless Debugging & QR Pairing] to pair your 
  Android 11+ phone wirelessly via QR code.
* Single-App Windows: Open [App Drawer & Cloner] to launch WhatsApp, banking, 
  or gaming apps into dedicated, borderless floating desktop windows.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ADB DOCTOR & 1-CLICK AUTO-REPAIR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If devices or ports encounter conflicts:
* Click [ADB Doctor & Fixer] -> [1-Click Auto-Repair All].
* Automatically terminates zombie processes, clears port 5037, restarts daemon, 
  and clamps Netd MTU to 1380 to eliminate VPN connection dropouts.
